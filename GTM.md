## The Opportunity in One Sentence

There is no purpose-built, AI-powered practice management tool for Ayurvedic practitioners in the US or Western markets — every practitioner today is hacking together generic health coach software that knows nothing about doshas, Prakriti, or classical Ayurvedic formulas.

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Primary: Solo US Certified Ayurvedic Practitioner
- Designation: CAP or CAAP (NAMACB certified)
- Full-time or near full-time practice
- Sees 5–20 patients/week; charges $200–600/initial, $100–150/follow-up
- Annual revenue: $60K–$180K
- Cash-pay only (no insurance billing needed)
- Currently using Practice Better, Jane App, or paper/Excel
- Based in Bay Area, NYC, LA, Austin, Denver, Chicago — or telehealth-first (growing fast)

### Secondary: Ayurvedic Wellness Clinic (2–5 Practitioners)
- Panchakarma centers, integrative wellness clinics
- Need multi-practitioner scheduling, Panchakarma room management
- Higher willingness to pay: $149–299/month

### Tertiary (Future): BAMS Diaspora Practitioners
- Indian-origin BAMS graduates practicing in US/UK/Canada/Australia
- Clinically sophisticated; frustrated by tools that don't understand Ayurvedic clinical language
- Bridge audience to eventual India market entry

## Pricing Model

| Tier | Price | Patient Limit | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seed** | $49/mo | 30 active patients | New graduates, AHCs, part-time |
| **Practice** | $89/mo | Unlimited | Solo CAP/CAAP full-time |
| **Clinic** | $149/mo | Unlimited + multi-user | 2–5 practitioners, Panchakarma centers |

- **Annual billing**: 15–20% discount (2 months free)
- **Add-ons**: SMS reminders (+$10/mo), white-label patient app (+$20/mo)
- **India/future**: Separate tier at $15–25/mo for BAMS diaspora / India market

**Pricing rationale**: Practice Better charges $69–155/mo with no Ayurveda features. Vaidya Care at $89/mo with full Ayurveda ontology + AI + patient portal is a clear upgrade proposition.

## Go-to-Market Phases

### Phase 1: Closed Beta (Months 1–3)
**Goal**: 10–20 paying beta practitioners, product validation, testimonials

- Reach out directly to Dr. Meenakshi's network and NAMA/AAPNA practitioners
- Offer Seed tier free for 3 months in exchange for structured feedback
- Focus on 5 practitioners using it with real patients, collecting case studies
- Identify the 3 most-requested features not yet built

### Phase 2: Soft Launch (Months 4–6)
**Goal**: 50–100 paying users, $4,000–$9,000 MRR

- Partner with 2–3 NAMA-accredited schools for student/graduate access
  - Target: The Ayurvedic Institute (Asheville), Kerala Ayurveda Academy, Southern CA University of Health Sciences
  - Offer: 50% off first year for new graduates
- Content: SEO blog targeting "Ayurveda practice management software," "dosha tracking for practitioners," "Ayurvedic patient portal"
- Launch on Product Hunt with an Ayurveda angle

### Phase 3: Growth (Months 7–12)
**Goal**: 200–400 paying users, $18K–$36K MRR

- **Sponsor NAMA Annual Conference** (May each year) — booth + demo
- Launch AAPNA partnership for member discounts
- Influencer outreach to senior Vaidyas with large followings (Dr. John Douillard / LifeSpa, alumni of Kripalu, Chopra Center)
- Activate Instagram/YouTube — "How Ayurvedic practitioners manage their practice" content series
- Add Panchakarma scheduling module to unlock Clinic tier sales

### Phase 4: Expansion (Year 2+)
- India / BAMS diaspora pricing tier
- Multi-language support (Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu for India market)
- Practitioner directory / patient-facing marketplace ("Find an Ayurvedic practitioner")
- White-label for wellness brands and Ayurvedic product companies

## Features That Win Deals

Based on market research, these are the features that will close sales:

1. **Dosha typing + Prakriti/Vikriti fields** — table stakes; generic tools don't have this
2. **AI assistant** — the wow demo moment; no competitor has it
3. **Patient portal with daily check-in** — the compliance problem every practitioner has
4. **Classical supplement library** (Triphala, Chyavanprash, Ashwagandha with full dosing ontology)
5. **Panchakarma scheduling** — unlocks the clinic segment and differentiates from Practice Better entirely
6. **Email portal link** — simple but critical: one click to send patient their portal URL
